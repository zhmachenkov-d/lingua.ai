## Product cards

### Duolingo

**Offer & features (shipping, evidence-backed):** Free core language path with game mechanics (energy/hearts, streaks, leaderboards). Multi-skill lessons (speak/read/listen/write). Expansion subjects: Chess, Math, Music. Paid: **Super** (no ads, Unlimited Energy, Monthly Streak Repair per App Store IAP copy); **Max** (AI Video Call / Roleplay lineage per 2023 blog; confirmed in 2025–26 user reviews). Recent trajectory: frequent App Store builds; IR still pushing “teach better / grow subscribers / expand beyond language”; Shanghainese course event on listing.

**Pricing & packaging:** Dollar amounts for Super/Max **not retrieved** this run (`duolingo.com/super` and support article returned empty). Packaging: Free → Super → Max (Max includes Super benefits per blog). Family plan mentioned by users (unverified pricing).

**Positioning:** Mass market, fun/free/effective education for anyone; IR: category leader / most downloaded language app (first-party). Kid-friendly UI; also used by adults (App Store narratives).

**Trajectory blip (≤6 mo):** Active ship cadence (App Store 7.14x, Sep–Oct 2026); IR calendar shows Q2 2026 results posted and Q3 2026 earnings call scheduled Nov 03, 2026. Non-language expansion (Chess/Math/Music) visible on listing.

**Customer voice:**
- **Praise:** Habit-forming, fun, free core works; Max Video Call builds speaking confidence (AmyBrown2829, 2025-12-09; Mila pascucci, May 29).
- **Complaints / friction (≤12 mo):** Ads annoying; streak repair via gems/pay (A12d4g, 2025-10-24). Max call can introduce unlearned verbs without mid-call hints (AmyBrown2829). Older 1★ (2022) on ineffective speaking/robot voice — outside freshness window.

| Lens | Assessment | Why (grounded) |
|------|------------|----------------|
| **Strengths** | Habit engine + free funnel + AI speaking tier | App Store positioning + Max reviews; IR organic/brand emphasis |
| **Weaknesses** | Depth vs fluency unclear; energy/ads monetization friction; not textbook-led or dictionary-first | User notes on fluency limits (tfowler13); ads/streak complaints; no dictionary/textbook claim on listing |
| **Borrow** | Streak/habit UX; optional AI conversation tier above base SR path; free valuable core | Proven engagement pattern in reviews |
| **Avoid** | Energy/hearts gating that trains pay-to-continue; gamification that overrides learner control of review | Cross-cited by Memrise reviewer who left Duo for forced path; Duo ads/streak pay pressure |
| **Applicable?** | **Partial** | Strong for habit + light AI feedback; weak fit for textbook-led adult English + dictionary vocab. Teacher-agent ≈ Max AI, not human tutor. |

## Cross-product patterns

1. **Freemium + AI speaking upsell:** Duo Max, Busuu Premium Plus, Memrise Pro all sell conversation/AI practice above a free or lighter paid tier (evidence: official + App Store copy).
2. **Habit mechanics vs learner control:** Streaks/paths drive retention but trigger backlash when they block on-demand review (Duo energy/streaks; Memrise path lock).
3. **Authenticity arms race:** Memrise (native video) and Busuu (community humans) differentiate against synthetic/gamey defaults; Duo counters with Max AI characters.
4. **SR is table stakes in study tools:** Quizlet names spaced repetition explicitly; language apps imply review hubs/algorithms without always branding SR.
5. **Textbook-led adult English is a white space among these four:** None lead with a named textbook spine + dictionary lookup + human/agent teacher feedback as the primary story (inferred from retrieved positioning only — absence is evidence of gap in *these* mass apps’ public offers).
