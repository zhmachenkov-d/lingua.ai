**Pricing:** Product page **Premium $159.99/year** ($13.33/mo). Free tier with limited AI features. FAQ also documents 7-day trial, Pro vs Premium channel complexity, and web lifetime/voucher offers (FAQ voucher figures look stale vs current Premium list — treat voucher $ as historical channel, not current list). Play: yearly/monthly memberships + 7-day trial noted.

**Positioning:** “World’s smartest Personal English AI Coach” / fluency & accent reduction for professionals and exam-takers; English-only target language (not multi-L2 like Speak).

**Trajectory blip:** Play update **2026-10-05** — new Professional Coaches (Business Storytelling, Communicate Like a Leader), paywall A/B, Android AI conversation audio fixes. Continues expanding from pronunciation drills into generative AI conversation + career coaches.

### ChatGPT tutors (category)

**Category definition researched:** OpenAI ChatGPT used as English/language tutor via (1) Study Mode, (2) Voice conversations, (3) custom GPTs / study prompts — **not a single SKU**. Peer SKU for market structure: **Speak.com** (below).

**Offer & features:** Study Mode — step-by-step / Socratic tutoring, knowledge checks, flashcard-style review, **upload textbook excerpts/notes/syllabus** (strongest “curriculum binding” signal in this set). Voice — spoken practice with plan-tiered limits; release notes show ongoing Live voice model updates. Custom GPTs — Plus feature for reusable tutor personas; release notes announce **planned retirement → plugins**. OpenAI itself warns Study Mode can make mistakes / behave inconsistently (instruction-tuned, not yet model-native pedagogy).

**Pricing:** Free includes Study Mode + limited Voice. Plus **$20/mo** and Pro **$200/mo** per release notes; pricing page confirms Study Mode on Free→Pro and Voice scaling Free→Pro Unlimited*. Go tier exists (geo/pricing details incomplete this run).

**Positioning:** General-purpose AI learning layer (“office hours”), not an English curriculum product. Study Mode aimed at students/homework; Voice enables conversation practice without dedicated pronunciation ASR scoring productization.

**Trajectory blip:** Study Mode launched **2025-07-29**; Help still iterating (updated days before access). Roadmap: progress tracking across chats, deeper personalization, train study behavior into main models. Parallel: custom GPT sunset risk; Voice+plugins expansion.

**Customer voice (≤12 mo):**
- **Praise:** Conversation practice when no partner; advanced models useful for rewriting/quizzes if user can verify (Reddit Sep 2025 thread).
- **Complaints:** Hallucinations on register/etiquette (“Thank thee…” anecdote); sycophancy on quizzes; grammar explanations unreliable for beginners; “fooling the ignorant” risk. Academic ESL feedback study: over-correction, level-inappropriate language, fabricated references.

| Lens | Assessment | Why |
|------|------------|-----|
| **Strengths** | Flexible conversation + Study Mode textbook upload; cheap Free entry; Voice practice | Official Help/Blog + pricing Study Mode on Free |
| **Weaknesses** | No trustworthy pronunciation scoring product; hallucination/sycophancy; no fixed English curriculum; GPT sunset churn | Reddit + OpenAI limitations + release notes |
| **Borrow** | Upload-bound tutoring to user’s materials; Socratic mode toggle; explicit “may be wrong” UX | Study Mode design |
| **Avoid** | Unverified grammar authority for beginners; relying on custom GPT store as durable product surface | Hallucination voice + GPT retirement path |
| **Applicable?** | **Yes as teacher-agent substrate / conversation layer; No as sole speak-ai product** | Best for feedback dialogue + material binding; must add scoring + curriculum elsewhere |

## Cross-product patterns

1. **Pronunciation scoring is the trust bottleneck.** ELSA’s differentiator (ASR scores) is also its loudest complaint (false excellent / forced shouting). Speak claims real-time pronunciation feedback with fewer public scoring-trust meltdowns in the sampled ≤12 mo reviews — but less syllable-drill depth evidenced. ChatGPT Voice practices conversation **without** a dedicated scoring product.
2. **Conversation AI is table stakes; curriculum binding is the split.** Speak ships expert curriculum + AI; ELSA ships path + role-play + exams; ChatGPT Study Mode binds to **user-uploaded** materials (textbook excerpts) but supplies no English course.
3. **Hallucination / feedback quality.** OpenAI documents Study Mode mistakes; Reddit 2025 warns against ChatGPT as grammar authority; ELSA users sometimes prefer ChatGPT conversation but distrust ELSA scores — neither stack is “trusted teacher” alone.
4. **Monetization friction clusters on ELSA** (lifetime upsell, refunds, dual Apple/web billing). Speak complaints skew feature/UI/price-value. ChatGPT monetizes via general plans ($20/$200) not English SKU.
5. **Russian UI:** Speak App Store UI language list **does not include Russian**. ELSA FAQ documents mother-tongue selection + bilingual tutor but **does not name Russian** in fetched pages; one App Store user mentioned a **Russian accent**, not Russian UI. **Not found = no confirmed Russian UI** this run.
6. **Textbook-led product gap:** Only ChatGPT Study Mode explicitly supports studying from uploaded textbook excerpts among the three; none market a bound commercial English textbook spine.
